Emails are coming to PrairieTest! Students will start receiving notifications about
their reservations and upcoming exams, giving them a little less to remember
and a little more room for what they’re studying.

This has been one of our most requested quality-of-life improvements, and
we’re excited to say that it will start being rolled out gradually to our partner institutions.

## A little less to remember

PrairieTest emails will track the whole exam life-cycle, helping students keep track of
what's next.

- **Ready to reserve:** when an exam becomes available for self-reservation:

(image of email goes here)

- **You're booked:** A confirmation when a reservations are made, with the exam’s time and place: 

(image of email goes here)

- **Coming up soon:** A reminder before the scheduled exam:

(image of email goes here)

## A change of plans

Students will also receive notifications when their reservations are changed or canceled.

If course or center staff moves a reservation to a different time or location, the email shows both the previous and 
updated details, so students can see exactly what changed.

(image of email goes here)

## Your inbox, your choice

Users can manage their notification configuration in the new **Email settings** section of their account settings:

(image of settings page goes here)

The notification address defaults to the account's institution email, but users can also verify a different address.

Notifications can also be turned off for most emails, except reservation changes made by course or center staff.

## See you in your inbox

Emails in PrairieTest are going to be slowly enabled, so keep an eye out! Once the feature is available 
at your institution, visit your account settings to check where your notifications will arrive and choose your preferences.

We’re excited to hear how this helps students, instructors, and testing centers keep track of exam plans. Let us know what you think!

We’ll help you remember the reservation. Studying is still up to you!
