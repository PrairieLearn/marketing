PrairieTest is built for secure testing. Hundreds of instructors rely on it, and our job has always been to keep it safe, fast, and dependable for them.

Part of that is the exam **Reservations** page, where you see each student's reservation for an exam. If you run exams in PrairieTest, you know the routine before one starts: who has booked, who hasn't, and which students need their session moved or a time extension fixed at the last minute. The page has all of that information, but in a large course it can be a long table to scan.

So we've reworked it. You can now search, sort, and filter the table, which makes the data a lot easier to work with, and edits save in place without reloading the page.

## Finding people

Type a name or UID into the search box to jump to a student. You can also type a session or testing center name to see everything booked there. Every column has a sort control, so put the table in whatever order suits what you're doing.

Two columns, **Session** and **Status**, now have filters, and they combine with search:

- **Session** lets you pick a course session, a testing center, or **Unassigned** for students who haven't been placed anywhere yet. Course sessions show their date, since sessions often share a name. Testing-center reservations are grouped under the center's name.
- **Status** narrows the table to Upcoming, In progress, Completed, or Missed reservations, or **None** for students who don't have a reservation at all.

The combination you'll probably reach for most is **Unassigned** plus **None**: that's your list of students who still need to book. A session plus **Upcoming** gives you a quick headcount for a session that hasn't started. The student count next to the table tells you how many rows match whatever you've set.

## Your view sticks around

Search, sort order, and both filters are written into the page URL. Reload and you're back where you were. You can bookmark the view, or paste the link to a colleague with access to the exam and they'll see the same thing.

**Clear filters** resets Session and Status but leaves your search and sort alone. **Clear search** only clears the search box.

## Editing without losing your place

The edit buttons beside a student's session, lock status, and time extension open a dialog. Save, and the row updates in place, with your filters intact. If the save fails, the dialog stays open with your changes and the error message, so you can fix the problem rather than start over.

You can also drag column widths, which helps when a long name or session title doesn't fit.

Students with pending invitations and students without a reservation are still listed alongside everyone else. Testing-center reservations follow the same rules as before; for example, their time extensions still can't be changed from the course page.

## Everything else is where it was

The **Actions for all students** menu hasn't changed: CSV import and export, random assignment, and the bulk lock, unlock, and delete actions are all still there.
