If you run exams in PrairieTest, you know the pre-exam routine: who's booked, who hasn't, who needs their session moved. The **Reservations** page has all of that, but in a big course it's a long table to scroll through.

So we've made it easier to find things.

## Search and filter

Type a name, UID, session, or testing center into the search box. Sort any column. Filter by **Session** (a course session, a testing center, or Unassigned) and by **Status** (Upcoming, In progress, Completed, Missed, or None).

The filters stack: Unassigned plus None is everyone who still needs to book, and a session plus Upcoming is a quick headcount.

Your search, sort, and filters are saved in the URL, so you can reload the page, bookmark it, or send the link to a colleague.

## Edit in place

Changing a student's session, lock status, or time extension now updates the row without reloading the page. If a save fails, the dialog keeps your changes and shows you the error. Columns are resizable too.

<BlogCalloutBox title="Filters and exam-wide actions" variant="info">
  Search and filters only change what you see. **Actions for all students** and
  **Export reservations** still apply to the whole exam.
</BlogCalloutBox>
