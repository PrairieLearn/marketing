## Find the students you need

Search by student name, UID, session, or testing center. Sort any column and resize it to fit.

- **Session** filters by course session, testing center, or **Unassigned**.
- **Status** filters by Upcoming, In progress, Completed, Missed, or **None**.

  src={sessionFilter}

<BlogImage

Combine **Unassigned** and **None** to find students without reservations. Search, sorting, and filters stay in the URL, so you can bookmark or share a view.

## Editing without losing your place

Use the edit buttons to change a student's session, lock status, or time extension. Changes save in place, and failed saves keep your draft. Testing-center editing rules are unchanged.

<BlogCalloutBox title="Filters and exam-wide actions" variant="info">
  Search and filters change what you see. **Actions for all students** and
  **Export reservations** still apply to the whole exam, including students
  hidden by your filters.
</BlogCalloutBox>

The update is rolling out now. Open an exam's **Reservations** tab to try it, and share feedback on [Slack](/slack).
