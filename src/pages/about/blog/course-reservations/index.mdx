Preparing an exam often means checking who has booked a session, finding students who still need a reservation, and making a few last-minute adjustments. Those tasks should be easy to do from one place.

We're updating PrairieTest's exam **Reservations** page with search, sorting, and filters to help course staff find the students they need. Reservation edits also update the table without a full page reload, so you can keep working in the same view.

## Find the students you need

Use **Search students or sessions** to look up a student by name or UID, or find reservations by their session or testing center name. Click a column's sort control to put the table in the order that helps with your next task.

The **Session** and **Status** columns now have filters that work together with search:

- **Session:** focus on a course session, a testing center, or students who are **Unassigned**. Course-session choices include the session date to help distinguish them. Testing-center choices group reservations under the displayed center name.
- **Status:** narrow the table to reservations that are Upcoming, In progress, Completed, or Missed, or choose **None** for students without a reservation.

For example, choose **Unassigned** and **None** to review students who still need to book. Or select a session and **Upcoming** to see its upcoming reservations. The student count shows how many rows match your current view.

## Return to the same view

Search, sorting, and the Session and Status filters are saved in the page URL. Reloading the page keeps that view, and you can bookmark it or share the link with another staff member who has access to the exam.

Use **Clear filters** to reset the Session and Status filters while keeping your search and sort order. **Clear search** resets the search separately.

## Make adjustments without losing your place

Use the edit buttons beside a student's session, lock status, or time extension to make changes. After a successful save, the table updates in place. If a save fails, the dialog keeps your draft and displays the error so you can correct it and try again.

Column widths can also be adjusted, giving longer student names or session details more room.

Students with pending invitations and students without reservations remain part of the table, alongside reservations in course sessions and testing centers. The existing rules for editing testing-center reservations still apply; for example, their time extensions cannot be changed from this course page.

## Keep exam-wide tasks in one place

The **Actions for all students** menu continues to provide CSV import and export, random assignment, and actions to lock, unlock, or delete reservations.
