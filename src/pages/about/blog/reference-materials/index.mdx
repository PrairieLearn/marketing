Instructors often allow students to bring their own notes, formulas, or diagrams to an exam; these are commonly called “cheat sheets”. Now instructors can allow students to upload these reference materials to PrairieTest before their exam. Students can then access them while taking the exam.

## Setting up an exam

As an instructor, you may enable this feature in the exam’s **Reference Materials** tab. You can configure when uploads open, set an optional closing deadline, and choose whether to allow PDFs or just images. Uploads always close when each student starts their exam, even if the deadline has not yet passed. Course staff can also allow PDFs just for individual students who need them as an accommodation. This permission applies to that student across all exams in the course that use reference materials.

### Uploading and viewing materials

Students will see the option to upload files as **Reference materials** on the reservation page for each specific exam. They can select images from their computer, use the computer camera, or scan a QR code and upload images directly from their phone.

Once the exam starts, students can view their materials while no longer being able to add or remove the files. And while taking an exam, they cannot access materials uploaded for other exams.

### Reviewing uploads

Course staff can review students’ materials from the exam’s **Reference materials** tab. They can upload or remove files on a student’s behalf, even during an exam. After deleting a file, it remains visible to staff and can be restored.
