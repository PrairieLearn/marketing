Instructors often allow students to bring their own notes, formulas, or diagrams to an exam; these are often called “cheat sheets”. Now instructors can allow students to upload these reference materials through to PrairieTest before their exam and access them while taking it.

## Setting up an exam

As an instructor, you must enable this feature in the **Reference Materials** tab on the exams settings . You can configure the specifics such as when uploads open, if they close at a specific time or when each student starts their exam, and if you want to allow PDFs or just images. For students who need PDF access as an accommodation, course staff can allow PDFs individually. This permission applies to that student across all exams in the course that use reference materials.

**Uploading and viewing materials**

Students will see the option to upload files as **Reference materials** on the reservation page for each specific exam. They can select images from their computer, use the computer camera, or scan a QR code and upload images directly from their phone.

Once the exam starts, students can view their materials while no longer being able to add or remove the files. And while taking an exam, they cannot access materials uploaded for other exams.

**Reviewing uploads**

Course staff can review students’ materials from the exam’s **Reference materials** tab. They can upload or remove files on a student’s behalf, even during an exam. After deleting a file, it remains visible to staff and can be restored.
