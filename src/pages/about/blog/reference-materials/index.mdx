Reference materials are now available in PrairieTest! Students can upload their permitted notes, formula sheets, and diagrams before an exam and access them directly from their reservation page during the exam.

Instructors control the upload window and whether PDFs are allowed. Course and testing center staff can review materials and help manage files on each student's behalf.

## Uploading reference materials

When an exam allows reference materials, students will find a **Reference materials** section on their reservation page. They can select multiple files, take a photo with their computer's camera, or click **Use phone** to upload photos from a phone.

For handwritten notes, the phone option makes preparation straightforward: scan the QR code, take photos or choose images, and wait for the upload confirmation. The photos appear in the student's collection on the computer, ready to review. JPEG, PNG, HEIC, and HEIF images are supported; PDFs can also be uploaded when the instructor permits them.

Students can drag their files into a convenient order and click an image thumbnail to check that every formula and diagram is readable. The homepage shows upload deadlines alongside reservations and reminds students about upcoming exams that do not yet have materials uploaded.

## Configuring exam settings

Course staff configure the feature from the exam's **Reference materials** tab. Enable materials, choose when uploads open, and close uploads either at a specific date and time or when each student starts their exam.

Images are supported by default. Instructors can allow original PDFs for everyone taking an exam, or grant a student PDF permission through the course's **Accommodations** settings. That individual permission applies to all exams in the course that allow reference materials.

The same tab lists each student's materials. Expand a student's collection to inspect files, add a missing page, or remove a file. Deleted files stay visible to staff and can be restored while they remain unexpired. Authorized testing center staff can manage the same collection through the student's reservation, including when the student's own uploads are locked.

## Viewing materials during an exam

Students access their references from the reservation page they use to start their exam. Images open in a separate viewer tab with thumbnails and a continuous scrolling view. Students can navigate between pages, zoom in on details, and rotate images. PDFs open separately in the browser's PDF viewer.

Starting an exam locks the student's ability to add or remove files, while leaving reordering available. Exam security restrictions still apply, including required LockDown Browser and testing center network settings. Staff can help manage files without reopening uploads for the student.

## Getting started

Open an exam in PrairieTest, select **Reference materials**, and enable the feature. Set the upload window and PDF permissions, then tell students what notes and references your course allows. Students can prepare their materials from their reservation page before exam day.

For detailed instructions, see the PrairieTest documentation for [students](https://us.prairietest.com/pt/docs/students/reference-materials), [course staff](https://us.prairietest.com/pt/docs/course/reference-materials), and [testing center staff](https://us.prairietest.com/pt/docs/center/reference-materials).
